# Welcome to NSCC SRM 👩‍💻👨‍💻

[![Contributors](https://img.shields.io/github/contributors/nscc-srm?color=brightgreen)]()
[![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/nscc-srm?color=yellow)]()
[![Forks](https://img.shields.io/github/forks/nscc-srm?color=blue)]()
[![Issues](https://img.shields.io/github/issues/nscc-srm?color=red)]()

The **Newton School of Coding Club (NSCC) at SRM** is the **leading technical club on campus**, igniting passion for coding, innovation, and technology since **November 2022**.  

With a community of **250+ students**, we thrive on **learning, building, and competing**. From college events and hackathons to real-world projects, NSCC members always showcase **creativity and innovation**.  

We collaborate with organizations like **DRDO, Microsoft, and The Newton School** to gain hands-on experience and bring projects to life.  

Join us as we **explore, innovate, and code the future** — at NSCC, **tech isn’t just a hobby, it’s a passion!**  

---

## 🌟 Who We Are & What We Do

We are the **Newton School of Coding Club - SRM IST**, passionate about:  

- Web Development 🌐  
- AI / ML 🤖  
- Cloud ☁️  
- DevOps ⚙️  
- Open Source 🌍  

We **organize workshops, hackathons, ideathons, and coding challenges**, building a **strong community of learners, coders, and innovators**.  

Our **main flagship event**: **HackNova** ⚡  

Additionally, we:  

- 🌱 Guide beginners into coding & problem-solving  
- 🛠 Build innovative projects & open-source contributions  
- ⚡ Host workshops, hackathons, and coding competitions  
- 🤝 Foster a collaborative culture where learning never stops
